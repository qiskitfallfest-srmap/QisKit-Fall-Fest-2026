Dear Students,

Next week our campus hosts IBM Qiskit Fall Fest 2026, held as an IBM Partner Plus event. For five days, 26 to 30 October, researchers, industry speakers and students will be working on quantum computing right here at SRM University-AP.

Quantum computing is moving from research labs into industry, and the students who build an early, hands-on understanding of it will have a real head start. You do not need any prior background in quantum physics. The workshops start from the basics, and the games and debates are designed for first-years as much as final-years.

I encourage every one of you to take part. The General Pass costs ₹99 for all five days, and it gives you access to everything listed below.
